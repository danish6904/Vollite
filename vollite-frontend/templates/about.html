About volLite

volLite – AI-Based Memory Forensics Assistant is a final year engineering project developed by Spundan Bhagat, Renu Khade, and Danish Ahmad.

Project Overview

In the field of cybercrime investigations, memory forensics plays a crucial role because attackers often hide malicious behavior in volatile memory (RAM) rather than on disk. Traditional tools like Volatility are powerful but require deep expertise and produce raw technical data that is hard to interpret quickly. Investigators often struggle with false positives, lengthy manual analysis, and lack of clear summaries.

volLite solves this problem by integrating classic forensic data extraction with artificial intelligence to automatically summarize findings, score risks, and provide proactive recommendations. It bridges the gap between raw forensic output and actionable insights that investigators, law enforcement, and security teams can immediately use.

Key Features

  • AI Summarization – Converts Volatility plugin outputs into human-readable summaries.
  • Suspicious Process Detection – Flags anomalies such as encoded PowerShell commands, DLL injection, and abnormal parent-child process relationships.
  • Risk Scoring – Calculates a percentage score (green → red) to prioritize threats.
  • Process Tree Visualization – Shows a color-coded hierarchy of processes to easily trace suspicious behavior.
  • Proactive Alerts – Provides recommended next steps (e.g., “Kill process”, “Quarantine system”).
  • Report Export – Generates HTML/PDF reports with clear sections: “What Happened” and “What To Do Next”.

Importance

By making forensic analysis faster and easier to understand, volLite helps investigators detect threats like ransomware, insider attacks, and advanced malware more efficiently. It reduces human error, saves time, and ensures that even non-experts can understand and act on forensic findings.

Future Scope

  • Integration with offline/private AI models for secure environments.
  • Live RAM capture and real-time analysis during ongoing cyberattacks.
  • Expansion beyond RAM to include registry keys, event logs, and persistence mechanisms.
  • Custom-trained forensic AI models for higher accuracy and domain-specific detection.
